## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Sky-paper reset vertical-slice and route-rollout evidence
Canonical ordinary-WebGL acceptance SHALL gate the UI reset in two stages from one identified release payload: first the 402×874 Battle vertical slice in full and representative inset safe areas, then Bootstrap, Lobby, Battle, and Settlement across 360×800, 375×812, 402×874, and 430×932 full and representative inset viewports plus a real wide desktop host.

#### Scenario: Battle vertical slice is reviewed
- **WHEN** the first acceptance gate captures ready, active, paused, and selected-detail Battle states
- **THEN** the manifest and composited canvas prove the sky edge, warm paper, one soil-heavy stage, independent phase/Wave row, correct ContextTray mode, persistent NurseryTray and RefreshAction, role-font containment, 38–43 percent reference stage-height band, lower closeout, and recorded draw/hit targets with no old in-stage Wave path

#### Scenario: Reference-fidelity decision
- **WHEN** automated Gate A checks pass for the rebuilt 402×874 Battle
- **THEN** the capture remains pending until the user explicitly accepts its
  component anatomy and page composition beside the supplied reference; prior
  engineering manifests cannot substitute for that decision

#### Scenario: Four-route rollout is reviewed
- **WHEN** the second acceptance gate traverses Bootstrap, Lobby, Battle, Settlement, return, and retry on the supported viewport matrix
- **THEN** every capture records the same build, theme revision, complete ArtSet revision, and role-font identities and shows one coherent sky-paper-orchard treatment without clipped copy, unsafe ornament, mixed legacy art, fallback font, input drift, black frame, or host scroll dependency

#### Scenario: Concept and production dependencies are audited
- **WHEN** the accepted WebGL payload and source/runtime asset manifest are inspected
- **THEN** the supplied full-page reference remains evidence only, every production UI raster is text-free and deterministically owned, destination GUIDs and hashes are recorded, and the payload contains no alternate theme or compatibility rendering

#### Scenario: Ordinary WebGL gate completes
- **WHEN** both reset gates pass after `FruitDefense.Editor.ProjectSetup.SmokeValidate` and the required UI-focused tests
- **THEN** the handoff may claim ordinary-WebGL UI acceptance only and does not claim Douyin or WeChat conversion readiness
