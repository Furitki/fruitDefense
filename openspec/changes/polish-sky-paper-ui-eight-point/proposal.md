## Why

The user approved the earlier ImageGen-backed Battle Gate A but rejected the latest runtime candidate: the separately delivered light-green action master was acceptable, while the integrated runtime buttons became incorrectly dark; PC scaling could expose four black nine-slice seams; and focused/selected buttons drew four unwanted lines. A focused visual correction can restore the accepted direct master and remove those rendering defects without reopening the accepted layout, command, typography, or gameplay contracts.

## What Changes

- Preserve the approved 402×874 Battle composition, role typography, authoritative draw/hit geometry, and all gameplay/session behavior as the before baseline.
- Generate project-owned, text-free and icon-free directly replaceable ImageGen component masters for the fresher leaf-green action family, warm-paper page shell, metric capsule, standard/raised paper panels, recipe/tool card, line-free rounded-paper nursery slot, phase block, and soil-stage frame. Reuse one master only where semantic slots intentionally share the same anatomy and material.
- Replace those selected semantic surfaces only through hash-locked complete-component crop/alpha extraction, approved geometry-alpha masking when required, padding, alpha-safe resize, measurement, hashing, and export; do not create decomposition sheets or solid-background correction generations, and remove the superseded generated/material source path rather than retaining a fallback.
- Keep labels and icons separate from raster surfaces, preserve destination `.meta` GUIDs and the finite 56-slot ArtSet contract, and bump the single active ArtSet/theme revision.
- Treat every explicitly supplied or user-approved reference-image visual parameter as P0 authority for the affected visual scope. Generic visual-system guidance fills only parameters that the reference does not define, or designs that have no reference image.
- Pair the accepted light leaf-green primary/secondary surface with soil-brown text and icons so rendered content contrast remains at least `4.5:1` without darkening or recoloring the generated master.
- Resolve contrast failures against a reference-authoritative container by changing the separate text/icon content token first. If both container and content are reference-locked and cannot satisfy a mandatory gate together, stop for user direction instead of changing the raster.
- Remove the action-specific `marker.selected` pointer/focus badge and use the existing restrained theme motion for a small contained press/focus response; retain the reviewed Battle header and nursery-section panel frames plus one line-free rounded-paper carrier for every header metric and nursery slot. Give `surface.metric` a dedicated master instead of reusing the bordered panel master, remove rejected solid/dashed linear rails and legacy/foreign-mask checkerboard fringes, and make nine-slice rendering seam-safe across the PC device-scale matrix.
