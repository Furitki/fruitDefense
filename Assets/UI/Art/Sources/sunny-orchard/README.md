# `sunny-orchard` editable masters

This is the owned master location for the approved A「阳光果园」production direction.

The 35 checked-in SVG files and six built-in ImageGen PNG masters own the 41 unique local exports. The PNGs provide four action-glyph silhouettes plus the two compact-control surfaces; deterministic SVGs provide the remaining library, including retry, return, refresh, and the transparent-center gameplay-stage frame. `icon.control-continue`, `icon.control-start-wave`, and `icon.control-start` intentionally share the same play-symbol master and Sprite. The current 56-slot manifest explicitly lists the 13 shared painted composition and target-tier bindings; slots 53-55 are local to this ArtSet and every slot remains independently serialized and validated.

- `art_manifest.json` records every slot, owned master/export path, SHA-256, stable GUID, dimensions, source scale, geometry, slice border, safe inset, and final-PNG optical inset.
- `prompt-record.json` records the built-in ImageGen prompts, reviewed references, selected outputs, transparency contract, deterministic white-mask transform, and source hashes for the four PNG glyph masters and slots 53-54.
- `export_sunny_orchard.py` is the dependency-light deterministic export pipeline. It renders the SVG recipes to antialiased PNG with Pillow, normalizes all action-glyph pixels to a strict white alpha mask, creates stable Unity importer metadata when missing, updates only the action-glyph `userData` contract on existing metadata, updates the ArtSet asset, and creates the review gallery. GUID and importer geometry are preserved.
- The exporter measures `optical_inset` for every binding from its final runtime PNG at `alpha >= 48` and writes the same four-sided value to ArtSet `opticalInset`. Shared painted bindings are remeasured from their owned runtime PNG instead of trusting copied source dimensions. `safeInset` remains the interaction/content protection contract; `opticalInset` is the visible-ink contract used for optical alignment.
- Source and runtime filenames use the same lowercase kebab-case semantic basename.
- The 2× source scale produces 128×128 nine-slice surfaces with 32 px protected borders and 96×96 common icons with 12 px transparent safe insets.
- `action.primary` uses the accessible semantic container `#436C15`; `action.danger` uses `#9F302B`. Against warm-white content `#FFF6E0`, their flat content fields measure `5.7477:1` and `6.6547:1`. The broader leaf-green `#6DBE4B` and red `#D34E45` remain non-container accent tokens.
- Common 96 px icons are optically normalized in the vector recipes: alpha-mass centroid stays within 4 px per axis, the common-family major dimension stays in 60–72 px, and drag cues keep at least a 64 px alpha short edge.
- Slots 30-36 and 38-39 are tintable action glyphs. Their seven unique masters are `pause`, shared `continue/start-wave/start`, `speed`, `retry`, `return`, `close`, and `refresh`. PNG and SVG masters plus runtime PNGs use `#FFFFFF` for visible RGB and contain no baked role/state color; `icon-tool-pot` and every resource icon remain intrinsically colored and outside this contract.
- Compact-control slots 53-54 deliberately stay simpler than the general component family: inactive and active are matching complete filled rounded-square surfaces with quiet cream centers. Inactive owns one soil-brown outline; active replaces it with one amber outline. Leaves, flowers, nodes, dots, inner frames, detached ornaments, layered overlays, and center texture are not part of this contract.

Do not crop or publish the approved style board as component artwork. Re-export in place, preserve destination `.meta` GUIDs, review the gallery, and increment the ArtSet revision whenever the visible production content changes.
